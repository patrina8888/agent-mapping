## 1. Executive thesis

The v1 map counted software-TAM and labor-TAM as two additive numbers and, by doing so, systematically inflated "agent displacement" by 60–93% in every outcome-priced category; **v2's coupling correction collapses them into one contested pool — realized vendor TAM = capture-rate × displaced-labor — and the entire investable thesis becomes a bet on capture-rate *expansion*, not on summing two big columns.** Once that lens is applied, the single sharpest fact v1 could not see appears: **revenue, delivered value, and displacement have decoupled** — coding is the most-proven agent market by ARR (~$5–9B, Cursor $4B, Claude Code ~$2.5B) and the least-proven by delivered labor value (METR −19%, realized coupled capture ≈0.1–0.2%), so the money is real and the displacement is nearly absent. Second, v1 mistook funding velocity for adoption; v2 shows deployment lags capability by an order of magnitude everywhere (MIT 95% zero-P&L, S&P 42% abandonment, <15% to production), so every "Conv=High" claim not corroborated by *production* evidence carries an explicit discount. Third, v1 treated the fastest-funding startup layer — securing, governing, identifying, and paying agents — as the frontier, when it is a **hostile software-TAM with no wage-bill denominator**, already being colonized by incumbents (Microsoft Agent 365, ServiceNow Control Tower) and acquired out pre-maturity (NHI: Astrix→Cisco, Oasis→Cyera), while the only place real displacement shows up in *audited* numbers is the boring end — services-arbitrage / BPO operator P&L. **The v2 conclusion HTC underwrites: the proven anchors (coding, CX) have closed their seed doors at lab-scale valuations and accrue to model-labs; the durable early-signal alpha sits in coupled, outcome-priced, incumbent-un-ownable cells — insurance claims, autonomous-SOC adjacencies, vertical voice, and the embodied-data layer — priced on the *displaced human outcome*, never on a software seat.**

## 2. The three structural findings

### Finding 1 — Revenue ≠ delivered value ≠ displacement. The whole thesis is capture-expansion, not addition.

The coupling rule is the spine: for any outcome/labor-denominated category, model **one contested pool** = human-retained + vendor-captured (capture × displaced) + disappeared surplus. Vendor realized TAM is **capture-rate × displaced-labor**; SW-ARR and the labor line are two views of one quantity and are **never summed**. Seat/token augment-copilots on a durable human are the exception — additive Expansion, never netted against a wage bill.

**Load-bearing evidence (coding, the anchor):**
- Revenue is the most-corroborated cluster in the dataset — Cursor **$1B (Nov-25) → $2B (Feb-26) → ~$4B (Jun-26)**, $60B SpaceX/xAI acquisition closed **Aug 14, 2026**; Cognition/Devin $492M ARR at $26B (→$40B talks). Verified.
- Delivered value is not: METR RCT **−19%** (CI +2/+39), perception inverted (+20% believed); the Feb-2026 follow-up redesigned the experiment and disclosed selection bias rather than overturning it.
- Against the ~$0.9–1.3T W1.1 pool, $5–9B seat-ARR implies **<1% capture** — and ~90% of it is **additive** seat revenue on still-employed senior engineers (senior postings +15%, total dev employment rising). Reading that as displacement is the recurring **~10× error**. Realized *coupled* coding capture ≈ **0.1–0.2%**.

**Map-wide rule:** revenue-capture sizes the additive/vendor line (Expansion when the human is durable); measured-productivity/headcount sizes the displacement line; they are different columns. The identical trap flags W2.2 (analyst copilots), W3.3 (AE copilots), W1.3 (close software), and all seven W7 relational rows — all additive, all Expansion, none to be netted.

### Finding 2 — The pilot-to-production chasm + capture-lag are the dominant facts of 2026.

Deployment lags capability by ~an order of magnitude, corroborated across four independent evidence types: **MIT NANDA 95% zero-P&L; S&P 42% abandonment (up from 17%); Gartner >40% cancellation; <15% reach production**; ~11–14% of MCP servers productionize; <10% of Agentforce customers past POC. Realized capture is **<1–11% even in the proven tracks** (CX 7–11%, RCM 0.3–0.6%, close 0.4%, machine-tending <1.5%, coding 0.1–0.2%). The web-verified METR horizon reinforces it: **~2h17m at 50% reliability** — and the **80%-reliability horizon is far shorter**, with messy real-world computer-use (OSWorld-2.0 long-horizon) still **~20%**. "Runs for a day" is true only for well-scoped, verifiable, code-shaped work. **Every Conv=High track not backed by production (not POC/bookings) evidence carries a discount; funding velocity is deployment-spend, not capture.**

### Finding 3 — The barbell: infra-funding is a hostile SW-TAM; services-arbitrage is the cleanest displacement.

The two ends of the 2026 funding distribution point opposite ways.
- **Hostile end (fastest-funding):** agent security/governance ($500M+; Zenity + Obsidian $210M in 48 hours), non-human identity, training/eval/runtime infra, payments rails. **None has a wage-bill denominator** → parallel `[$]` SW-TAM ledger, never a labor track. Inside SW-TAM it is *incumbent-captured*: Microsoft Agent 365 at $15/user/mo + ServiceNow Control Tower colonizing; NHI acquired out (Astrix→Cisco, Oasis→Cyera ~$1B) before independent maturity; x402 real settled volume **~$41M vs report-mill "$50B."** It fails twice — not labor, and not a durable independent software company.
- **Clean end (best-corroborated real displacement):** services-arbitrage / BPO — the only place displacement shows up in **audited operator guidance**, not press releases. Web-confirmed 2026-09-03: **Teleperformance FY26 guidance 0–2% LFL, Q1 −2.2% LFL, targeting all ~500k employees on AI by end-2026/early-2027, efficiency savings raised to €150–170M**; **Concentrix cut FY26 revenue to $9.93–10.03B and EPS to $10.83–11.18**; Bloomberg "uninvestible" (Jun 30, 2026); Philippines IBPAP quietly cut its 2028 ceiling. The catch (Finding 1 again): the app-layer giants that "won" this (Sierra $15.8B, Decagon $4.5B/~130×) are lab-scale and seed-closed, and the value accrues as **incumbent margin compression + consolidation**, not a fresh seed category.

**The durable exception inside the hostile end is autonomous SOC (W1.8)** — agents *doing the analyst job*, not securing agents — the one infra-adjacent cluster with a nameable, chronically understaffed occupation.

## 4. Top 5 anti-consensus calls

**1. "AI is replacing engineers." — Wrong on magnitude; the value accrues to labs, not app-layer startups.**
Coding is the most-proven market by revenue and least-proven by delivered value (METR −19%, capture ~0.1–0.2%; seat ARR is additive Expansion). *Trade: do not underwrite any coding-agent seed/A as labor displacement. The app layer has consolidated to model-labs and strategics (Cursor→SpaceX $60B, Claude Code lab-owned, Copilot=Microsoft; L=5). The only HTC-shaped exposure is the coupled/outcome wedge or the eval/verification hedge — never a senior-augmentation seat play.*

**2. "BPO is dead — 15M jobs gone, uninvestible." — Half-wrong; the displacement is real but un-investable at the app layer.**
80% deflection is marketing (audited resolution 35–45%, capture 7–11%); the real signal is the *operator's income statement*, not the vendor's ARR; and it accrues as incumbent margin compression, not a startup category. *Trade: don't short the operators to zero (TP is pivoting to sell AI on flat revenue); don't chase horizontal CX (Sierra/Decagon seed-closed at lab scale). Hunt the vertical/voice adjacents and geo-arbitrage (LATAM/Africa) below the crowned giants.*

**3. "Securing/governing agents is the hot category." — It's a feature, not a company, and not even a labor track.**
No wage-bill denominator → `[$]` ledger, never summed; incumbent-colonized (Agent 365, Control Tower); NHI acquired out pre-maturity. *Trade: the fastest-funding layer of 2026 is the most hostile standalone TAM — pass on agent-security/identity/payments-rails seed rounds. The single exception is autonomous SOC (agents doing the analyst job), and even there the base case is feature-ization/acquisition <$1.5B.*

**4. "AI now runs the whole company." — The biggest node is infra, and most of the TAM is demand-creation, not displacement.**
Naïve is company-as-an-API infra, not an "agent CEO"; most TAM is `[L]` (solopreneurs, net-new micro-businesses) → Expansion, not Compression; with Builder.ai (700 engineers posing as AI) as the category's corpse and zero disclosed retained ARR, honest capture is ~0.01–0.02%. *Trade: if you play it, underwrite the ops-graph / agent-HR-directory-payroll primitive beneath the bundle or a vertical niche where retained data compounds — never the horizontal operator, which the labs will vertically integrate.*

**5. "AI eats offshore services — short TCS/Infosys, long an AMS-disruptor." — Inverted; the incumbents are the winners.**
They internalize AI into margin + pyramid-flattening (revenue-per-head up, fresher intake −80%, headcount ~flat, profits up); AI-native managed-delivery captures <$0.7B; the commodity-code floor is oss-deflating (DeepSeek V4). And services displacement is *bifurcating*, not uniform — GCCs hired ~510k AI roles in 2026. *Trade: there is no defensible seed AMS-disruptor lane; the correct posture is not a services short but selective exposure to the majors capturing the deflation, plus the software-factory control-plane / services-QA verification layer (the anti-C1 hedge).*

## 6. What to watch / kill criteria

**Four master leading indicators (the framework resolves on these):**

1. **Coupled-revenue mix (the single cleanest resolver).** Autonomous/outcome-priced coding ARR as a share of total coding-agent ARR. **≥25% → coding is becoming labor** (flip W1.1 toward Compression, raise displacement conviction). **<15% (seat-dominated) → augmentation confirmed** (lock W1.1a Expansion). *Base case: bear.*
2. **BPO operator guidance (the cleanest audited displacement).** Teleperformance + Concentrix organic growth and, decisively, **combined agent headcount**. **≥10% YoY headcount down + rev-per-head +15% → production seat-elimination confirmed.** Flat-to-up headcount + resolution ≤45% → it's a margin/mix story. *Watch H2-26 and FY27 guidance; TP's own €150–170M efficiency target and "500k on AI" plan are the tell.*
3. **W8 job-creation growth (the net-new offset + deployment-speed proxy).** Net-new agent-engineering wage bill (headcount × comp) toward ~$10B by Jan 2028; "AI/agent/context engineer" staying top-5 fastest-growing. **Kill signal: if W8 flatlines, the aggregate map loses both its net-new cushion *and* its leading indicator that W1–W7 are actually deploying** — treat the offset as transitional and re-underwrite the creation-side hunts (#5) toward eval/expert-data only.
4. **METR horizon (50% vs 80% reliability) + OSWorld-2.0 long-horizon.** The 50%-mark crossing ~8h is *not* the signal; the **80%-reliability horizon and multi-app OSWorld-2.0 staying <50%** is the binding gate. If long-horizon reliability breaks past ~50% before Q4 2027, the pilot-to-production discount compresses and every coupled track's capture-expansion timeline pulls forward — re-rate physical and back-office tracks.

**Universal kill criterion — the pilot-to-production discount.** Any position sized on a "Conv=High" claim without *production* (not POC/bookings) evidence is discounted until MIT/S&P-class abandonment reverses. Specifically kill or freeze a thesis when: (a) the cell's leading pure-play is **acquired out <$1.5B pre-scale** (NHI base rate — applies first to autonomous SOC and XBRL); (b) an incumbent core-system vendor ships the exact wedge natively at ~zero incremental price (insurance: Duck Creek/Guidewire at >20–30 carriers; close: BlackLine Verity; service-desk: ServiceNow Action Fabric); (c) a **Builder.ai/11x-style washing or churn blowup** lands in the cohort (company-in-a-box, SDR); or (d) the number driving the thesis is **valuation, bookings, work-units, funding, or vendor deflection-%** rather than net, recurring, definition-locked, production-confirmed, coupling-converted ARR.

**Bottom line for the IC:** underwrite capture-*expansion* on coupled, outcome-priced, incumbent-un-ownable cells — insurance claims first, SOC/voice/embodied-data next — and refuse every position priced on a software seat sold as displacement. The map's honesty is its edge: the proven markets are closed, the funded infra is hostile, and the alpha is in the four cells where the incumbent's moat provably does not transfer.
